Jev Launcher

A launcher for your Mac that understands what you mean.

Download

macOS 14 or later · 0.7 MB

How to use it

  1. Press ⌥ Space anywhere on your Mac.
  2. Type what you mean. The pdf I just downloaded. Wifi off. 15% of 240.
  3. Press Return when the green ↵ appears.
  4. Once, paste your TypeSafe API key into Settings, under the bolt in the menu bar.
